CRUMPLER BAG IT YOUR WAY BAG DESIGN

Reimagining everyday carry through a creative bag design concept, developed in collaboration with Crumpler owner David Roper and the Australian team for an ION Orchard showcase and potential sales launch.

PROJECT 02 — SINGAPORE × AUSTRALIA
01 / KEY VISUAL CRUMPLER CAMPAIGN CONCEPT

01 — The concept

Make it yours. Crumple it your way.

Winner of the CRUMPLE BAG IT YOUR WAY competition, this project explores the creative possibilities of personalising everyday carry through a distinctive bag design concept for Crumpler. The project celebrates individuality, experimentation and the brand's unconventional approach to functional design.

Developed in collaboration with Crumpler owner David Roper and the Australian team, the concept explores how a creative campaign could translate into a physical retail experience at ION Orchard, Singapore, with the potential for a future sales launch.

Bringing together product design, visual storytelling and retail presentation, the project aims to connect Crumpler's distinctive identity with a new audience through an engaging, experience-led showcase.
