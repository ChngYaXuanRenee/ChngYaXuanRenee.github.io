01 — The concept

Reconstructing a medieval environment through digital modelling.

Ancient Room is a 3D environment project focused on the design and construction of a medieval-inspired interior. The environment combines architectural modelling, modular assets and decorative details to create an immersive room and hallway experience.

The project began with visual research into medieval interiors, architectural references, materials and historical objects. References were organised in PureRef to establish a visual direction and support decisions around proportions, textures and environmental details.

The environment was developed through a combination of MAYA and ZBrush. Low-poly structures were first created to establish the overall spatial composition before moving into more detailed modelling and sculpting.

Materials and textures were developed in Adobe Substance Painter before the final environment was brought into UNITY, where lighting, mapping and baking were adjusted for the real-time presentation.

03 — Documentation & process

From reference research to a playable environment.

I began by collecting references for medieval interiors, hallways, architectural structures, asset items and material textures such as bronze and brick. These references were organised in PureRef to establish a visual reference library.

I then planned the proportions and measurements of the hallway and room, while researching historical references to inform the environmental details and creative interpretation.

Rough sketches and concept ideas were used to visualise the overall environment before beginning the whitebox. I developed low-poly models for the main structure, hallway and modular assets before progressing into a more detailed whitebox.

Once the overall structure was established, I developed the detailed modelling and decorative assets. More complex elements were transferred into ZBrush for additional sculpting and detail.

The final stages involved UV mapping, texturing, lighting, mapping and baking before bringing the environment into UNITY. A third person controller was used to present the environment as an explorable real-time space.
