01 — The concept

Making recycling more engaging, one small action at a time.

BinGo is an interactive app concept that transforms recycling into a fun, rewarding and educational experience. Instead of treating sustainability as a routine task, the app encourages users to learn about recycling through bite-sized lessons, interactive quizzes and a points-based reward system.

Users can test their recycling knowledge, track their progress and earn rewards, from reusable items to community recognition. These features are designed to make learning feel more accessible while giving users a sense of achievement as they build environmentally conscious habits.

The design process involved researching user behaviours and pain points, exploring gamified solutions, mapping user journeys and developing wireframes. The UX framework and task flow helped organise the experience, while the design system established a consistent visual direction across the app.

The final concept brings education, motivation and interaction together in one digital experience. By breaking sustainability into smaller and more rewarding steps, BinGo aims to show how everyday actions can contribute to a greener future.
