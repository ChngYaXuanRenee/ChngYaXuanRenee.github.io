01 — The research

Turning market understanding into user-centred opportunities.

During my research for Eezee, a B2B startup, I conducted a market analysis focused on Indonesia to understand the business landscape, employment regulations and competitive positioning. Using a structured approach, including PESTEL analysis, competitor analysis and SWOT analysis, I examined key market factors that could influence user needs and digital interactions.

By analysing industry pain points, procurement behaviours and digital adoption, I translated market insights into potential UX strategies. This helped identify considerations for platform localisation, interface improvements and user flows tailored to Indonesian business users.

The research connects broader market conditions with the practical experience of using a B2B platform. It highlights how understanding local business practices and user needs can inform design decisions, helping make a digital product more relevant to its intended market.

The findings support a user-centred approach to improving the platform's usability and overall experience in Indonesia, with research serving as a foundation for future design recommendations rather than treating interface decisions in isolation.
