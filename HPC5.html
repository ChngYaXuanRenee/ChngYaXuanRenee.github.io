SEMBCORP SIWW.

Art direction and exhibition design for Sembcorp's showcase at Singapore International Water Week, translating sustainability and innovation into a physical visual experience.

PROJECT 04 — SINGAPORE
01 / FEATURED SHOWCASE SEMBCORP · SIWW 2024

01 — The project

Communicating sustainability through space and visuals.

I managed the art direction and design of Sembcorp's showcase booth for Singapore International Water Week (SIWW), an event bringing together leaders and experts in water and urban sustainability. The showcase welcomed Minister for Sustainability and the Environment Ms Grace Fu, alongside utility professionals and city leaders.

The process began with understanding the brief, the audience and Sembcorp's brand values. I explored how exhibition graphics and visual storytelling could communicate sustainability, innovation and the company's role in shaping more sustainable urban environments.

Water and flow informed the visual direction, expressing adaptability and interconnectedness. I also worked with stakeholders to align the design with the intended message, refining the presentation through feedback and attention to detail.

This project strengthened my ability to translate a corporate message into a physical visual experience, balancing brand consistency, audience engagement and the practical demands of a public exhibition.
