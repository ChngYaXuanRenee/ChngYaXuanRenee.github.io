The Missing Archive

The M SS NG Archive reframes absence as evidence. By collectivising individual experiences of censorship, the campaign transforms what platforms dismiss as minor moderation decisions into an undeniable, documented pattern of systemic bias. Not just a tool for our championed cause, we place emphasis on the individuals impacted by suppression — especially the generation of young women developing their own bodily awareness and autonomy. We provide a platform to not just women's rights organizations and social campaigns, but also the everyday woman to understand she is not alone. We are the first step to real change — to make suppression undeniable.
