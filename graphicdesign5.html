THE MISSING ARCHIVE.

Reframing absence as evidence. The Missing Archive brings individual experiences of censorship together to reveal patterns of digital suppression and create space for women's voices to be seen, documented and heard.

PROJECT — CAMPAIGN
01 / CAMPAIGN COVER THE MISSING ARCHIVE — 2026

01 — The concept

Making suppression undeniable.

The Missing Archive reframes absence as evidence. By collectivising individual experiences of censorship, the campaign transforms what platforms dismiss as minor moderation decisions into an undeniable, documented pattern of systemic bias.

Rather than treating censorship as an abstract platform issue, the project places emphasis on the individuals impacted by suppression — especially young women developing their own bodily awareness and autonomy. When content disappears, the experience and the person behind it can disappear from public view as well.

The campaign aims to provide a platform not only for women's rights organisations and social campaigns, but also for everyday women to share their experiences and recognise that they are not alone. By making these experiences visible, The Missing Archive takes a first step towards making suppression harder to ignore.
