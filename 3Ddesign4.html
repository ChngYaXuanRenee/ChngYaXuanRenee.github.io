Candy Land.

01 — The concept

Bringing a candy-coloured world into augmented reality.

Candy Land is an interactive augmented reality project centred around an ice cream truck designed for a playful, candy-inspired environment. The project explores how 3D modelling, digital illustration and interactive technology can work together to create an engaging visual experience.

I modelled the ice cream truck in Autodesk Maya and textured it in Substance Painter. To build the surrounding environment, I designed a two-dimensional background in Adobe Illustrator, combining illustrated scenery with a three- dimensional object.

The completed assets were imported into Unity as part of the AR development process. Using Adobe Aero's interaction panel, I configured animations and interactions that allowed the truck to move automatically and jump when tapped by the user.

By combining a static illustrated backdrop with an animated 3D object, the project explored how simple interactions could make a digital environment feel more playful and responsive.

03 — Documentation & process

From a 3D model to an interactive experience.

The project began with the design and modelling of an ice cream truck in Autodesk Maya. I developed the truck as the main three-dimensional asset, considering its overall form and visual character within the playful Candy Land concept.

I then applied textures in Substance Painter to develop the truck's surface appearance. Alongside the 3D asset, I created a two-dimensional illustrated background in Adobe Illustrator. This allowed me to combine different visual approaches within a single environment.

After preparing the assets, I brought the scene into the AR workflow using Unity and Adobe Aero. Through Adobe Aero's interaction panel, I set up behaviours that allowed the truck to move automatically and respond to a tap with a jumping animation.

The interaction introduced a simple element of surprise, encouraging users to engage directly with the truck rather than only viewing it as a static model. It also allowed me to explore how movement and user input could add another layer to a designed environment.

This project helped me understand how 3D asset creation, illustration and interactive technology can come together to create an augmented reality experience. It also strengthened my understanding of designing digital objects that respond to user interaction.
