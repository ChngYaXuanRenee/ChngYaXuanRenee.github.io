FOODHOOD App

FOODHOOD is more than just an app—it’s a community-driven platform that evokes memories of shared meals, family traditions, and the sensory delights of home-cooked dishes. Inspired by personal and collective nostalgia, it connects users through food stories, flavors, and traditions.

With the economic impact of Covid-19 leading to retrenchment, many turn to different avenues of work. This start-up is on a mission to develop software to help displaced workers in Singapore showcase their culinary skills and sell creations to the community. FOODHOOD allows anyone to order home-cooked food on demand.
