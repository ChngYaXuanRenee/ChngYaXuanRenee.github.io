BIOCODE LAB.

This project explores plant responses to touch through bio-digital experiments, translating biometric data and botanical sounds into generative visuals and audio using p5.js, TouchDesigner, and Arduino.

01 — The concept

Building a sushi restaurant, one card at a time.

This research document records an early acoustic experiment uncovering the undisclosed mysteries of an ancient botanical specimen discovered today. Bridging archaic botany and contemporary sound science, the project investigates the plant’s biological response through three distinct physical methods of exploration: water application, stem fracture, and surface pressure. By translating these interactions into core behavioral notations, Flow, Fracture, and Compression, it reveals an unseen, resonant sound vocabulary hidden within living plant tissue.

Our multi-stage framework bridges human vitals, botanical structures, and generative code into dynamic visual and acoustic outputs. First, we used p5.js to translate human breath, pulse, and blinks into geometric patterns mimicking leaf stomata, sap flow, and phototropism. Next, we fed these biometric inputs and plant acoustics into TouchDesigner, distorting 2D/3D grids and point clouds to simulate cellular stress, fluid movement, and structural tension. We then built audio-driven animations where procedural 3D forms deform in real time to a biological sound chord based on Flow, Fracture, and Compression. Finally, we used Arduino leaf contact sensors to track real-time electrical impedance and touch micro-reactions, completing a unified bio-digital study.
