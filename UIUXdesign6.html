01 — The concept

Making public transport more accessible, one journey at a time.

P1US is an inclusive public transport app designed to improve accessibility and independence for commuters, especially individuals with visual impairments, mobility challenges and other accessibility needs. The concept explores how thoughtful digital design can reduce barriers when navigating public transport.

By integrating features such as real-time bus and MRT arrival notifications, voice feedback and a card top-up system, P1US aims to make essential commuting information easier to access. These functions are designed to help users navigate their journeys with greater clarity, confidence and convenience.

The project focuses on making the app experience more intuitive by considering how different commuters access information and interact with digital interfaces. Rather than treating accessibility as an additional feature, P1US places inclusive usability at the centre of the proposed experience.

Through the interface design, the project explores how a more accessible transport app could support everyday mobility and help create a public transport experience that considers a wider range of user needs.
