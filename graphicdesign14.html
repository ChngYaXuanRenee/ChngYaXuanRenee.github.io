NOSTALGIC NOODLES.

Bringing the warmth of traditional prawn noodles to life through a brand identity inspired by familiar flavours, family memories and the comforting feeling of home.

PROJECT 02 — BRANDING
01 / BRAND PRESENTATION NOSTALGIC NOODLES — VISUAL IDENTITY

01 — The concept

A taste of home, told through a bowl of noodles.

Nostalgic Noodles centres on the comforting, flavourful experience of traditional prawn noodles, prepared with ingredients and techniques that reflect an older, cherished style of cooking. More than just food, each bowl tells a story of homemade meals, family gatherings and the love of a grandmother's kitchen.

The brand experience is designed as a sensory journey, capturing the familiar tastes, smells and textures that make Singaporean cuisine special. From the visual warmth of a hearty bowl to the aroma of a rich broth, every touchpoint aims to evoke a sense of comfort and nostalgia.

The identity pays tribute to family recipes and traditional cooking methods, translating these memories into a cohesive visual system across brand assets, promotional materials and digital applications.
