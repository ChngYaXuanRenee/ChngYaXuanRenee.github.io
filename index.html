02 / A little about me Curiosity is the starting point ✳

Hi,
I'm Renee.

I’m a Singapore-based designer interested in how design, technology and storytelling can shape the way people understand and experience the world.

My practice spans visual communication, UI/UX, digital media and creative experimentation. I enjoy translating complex ideas into clear, thoughtful and engaging experiences.

Currently pursuing Design Communication at LASALLE College of the Arts, I’m always looking for new perspectives, meaningful collaborations and opportunities to learn through making.
