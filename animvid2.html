01 / ANIMATION FILM ESCAPE WITH SLIME! — MOTION STUDY

01 — The concept

A playful escape into a world of slime.

Escape with Slime! explores the visual possibilities of animation through the playful character of slime. The project uses movement and visual storytelling to create an engaging moving-image experience.

By bringing the concept to life through animation, the project explores how motion can shape the viewer's experience, giving the visuals a sense of energy, personality and progression.

The final video brings the concept together into an animated piece, translating the initial idea into a visual experience through timing, movement and digital media.
