01 — The concept

When architecture begins to move.

Dancing Building explores the relationship between architecture and movement, imagining a built structure as something expressive rather than static. Through animation, the building becomes a subject that can shift, respond and develop its own rhythm.

The project uses motion to change how the viewer experiences an architectural form. Instead of observing a building as a fixed object, the animation invites us to see it through movement, timing and visual transformation.

The final video brings these ideas together into a moving-image experiment, using animation to give an ordinary structure a different kind of presence.
